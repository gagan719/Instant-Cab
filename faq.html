Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about fixed pricing, packages, and booking policies.

What is included in the Fixed Price?

All Instant Cab fixed prices include base vehicle hire, driver allowance, national & state highway toll charges, and GST. There are no sudden meter hikes or extra charges upon arrival.

How do Package Plans work?

Packages (such as Daily Saver, Monthly Commuter, and Business Pack) allow you to pre-purchase ride tokens at discounts up to 45%. When booking a ride, simply select "Use Active Package Ride" and 1 ride token is deducted with zero out-of-pocket payment needed.

What is the Local Ride Above 20 km feature?

Standard city taxi rules apply for trips up to 20 km. For longer suburban, industrial, or airport journeys above 20 km, Instant Cab applies special tiered distance slabs where the per-kilometer rate decreases progressively.

Can I cancel my ride?

Yes! Free cancellation is offered up to 1 hour prior to your scheduled pickup time directly from your booking details page. If you used a package ride, the ride token is automatically refunded back to your wallet.

How are drivers assigned?

Our automated system instantly checks driver location, vehicle category, and availability to assign a top-rated captain. You will see driver and vehicle registration details immediately on your confirmation screen.
